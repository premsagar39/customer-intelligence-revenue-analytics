# Business findings

The accepted data contains £19,013,836.27 net revenue from 40,077 positive-sale invoices. Gross revenue is £20,476,260.45; returns/credits total £1,462,424.18. Reconcile all three before interpreting growth.

Of 5,878 identified purchasing customers, 4,255 made at least two observed purchases (72.4%). This is repeat purchasing within the dataset window, not a causal retention program result. The highest-spend 588 customers account for 63.93% of identified gross purchase spend. Account review and service coverage for this concentrated group are proposed actions; no campaign uplift was measured.

Anonymous purchases account for 15.15% of gross revenue. Improving identity capture could expand customer analysis coverage, subject to business consent and privacy requirements; it would not automatically raise revenue.

The RFM snapshot identifies 708 at-risk customers with £1.52m historical gross spend. This is a prioritization pool for review, not forecast recoverable revenue. Champions include 1,268 customers with £11.80m historical spend.

Review market and product returns with transaction details before interventions. An unusually high return/gross ratio can reflect credits for purchases outside the observed period. Exclude final partial December when comparing complete-month trends.
